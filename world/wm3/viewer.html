The world as world model 3 sees it, rebuilt every 30 seconds from the simulator's logs: every driver at its exact place on the road graph, every open request, every road's travel time. Click a driver or a waiting rider to see its full record (what the model reads at this step) and what happens to it in the next 30 seconds (what the model must predict). Scroll to zoom, drag to pan.

Click a driver (dot) or a waiting rider (x) on the map.

Colours: grey idle; blue filled heading to a pickup, blue ring an offer is out; orange with a rider (ring: outside the city on an intercity trip); aqua repositioning; grey ring on a break. Black x: a rider waiting with no driver yet; blue line: driver to the rider it is driving to. Roads are violet where travel time is at least 1.15 x free-flow, darker = slower. Hidden driver traits (accept bias, compliance) are never shown: they are what the model has to infer from each driver's history.
